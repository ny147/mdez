Designing a workspace that opens quietly

Mdez should not greet writers with ceremony. It should open where the work is, keep the document stable, and make every surrounding control feel close without becoming loud.

The reader keeps a narrow column, long line height, and no controls inside the text. Navigation stays available at the edges so the writing remains the center of gravity.

What the shell protects

The soft surface keeps contrast steady without turning the workspace into plain white chrome. The gradient is present, but it behaves like stationery paper rather than decoration.

Mode behavior

  • Read mode uses a mint cue and a centered 680px column.
  • Edit mode switches to a lavender writing surface with a monospace rhythm.
  • Shelf view keeps books discoverable without covering the writing state.
