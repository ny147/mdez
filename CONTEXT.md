# Mdez workspace language

Mdez is a writing and reading workspace for organizing Markdown pages into books. This glossary records agreed product meanings.

## Language

**Bookmark**:
A personal mark for returning to a page within a particular workspace. It belongs to the reader in this browser, is not shared with other group members, and does not follow the reader to another device.
_Avoid_: Shared favorite, group bookmark

**Workspace search**:
A search across the books and pages in the current workspace, including Unsorted pages. Results identify their book and never include another workspace.
_Avoid_: Global search, book-only search

**Book**:
A top-level collection of pages in one workspace. A book cannot contain another book. Legacy nested books are preserved as flat books whose names include their former path, such as `Research / Sources`.
_Avoid_: Folder hierarchy, nested book, sub-book

**Unsorted pages**:
The fixed final Library collection for pages that do not belong to a book. Deleting a book moves its pages here without deleting or changing their content.
_Avoid_: Root folder, orphaned pages

**Resume page**:
The last page a reader opened in this browser within a particular workspace. It is personal history; another group member’s edits do not change it.
_Avoid_: Latest edited page, group activity

**Library companion**:
Mdez's illustrated mascot, a decorative character that welcomes readers and adds personality to their library. It is not an interactive assistant or a source of document advice.
_Avoid_: AI assistant, writing assistant, chatbot
